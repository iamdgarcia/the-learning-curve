# The Secret History of the Moon Base

## Overview
The Moon Base, known as "Luna Prime," was established in 2042 by a coalition of nations. It is located in the Shackleton Crater at the lunar south pole.

## Purpose
The primary purpose of Luna Prime is to mine Helium-3, a clean energy source for fusion reactors on Earth. It also serves as a gateway for missions to Mars.

## Inhabitants
There are currently 150 permanent residents on Luna Prime. They are scientists, engineers, and hydroponic farmers.

## Security Protocols
Access to the fusion core is restricted to Level 5 personnel. The base is protected by an automated defense system known as "The Aegis."

## Fun Facts
- The gravity is 1/6th of Earth's.
- The favorite sport is "Low-G Basketball."
- The base commander is Captain Daniel Garcia.
