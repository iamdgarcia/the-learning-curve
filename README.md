| Demo | What it does | Tech | Open |

|---|---|---|---|

| **Jev Mini Bench** | Pocket explorer of the tiny decision-model wave (kev, tev, gev, clef…): feel the 1200 ms vs 12 ms latency gap, try a demo router, vote your favorite repro | Vanilla JS, mobile-first | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-jev-mini-bench.static.hf.space) |

| **Voice Metrics Dashboard** | Paste a voice-agent transcript JSON → KPIs (talk-time, gaps p95, interruptions, containment), turn timeline & gap histogram | Chart.js, 100% client-side | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-voice-metrics-dashboard.static.hf.space) |

| **Word Timing Explorer** | Whisper word-level timestamps in-browser: record or upload, click a word to seek, watch playback highlight — the substrate for barge-in & alignment | transformers.js (WebGPU/WASM), 100% client-side | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-word-timing-explorer.static.hf.space) |

| **Latency Gap Simulator** | Where the milliseconds go in a voice-agent turn: waterfall of endpointing → ASR → LLM TTFT → TTS, presets from robust to S2S duplex | Vanilla JS, teaching approximation | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-latency-gap-simulator.static.hf.space) |

| **Kokoro Tab** | 82M-param Kokoro TTS synthesizes in your tab — zero backend, time-to-first-audio meter | transformers.js/kokoro-js (WASM), 100% client-side | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-kokoro-tab.static.hf.space) |

| **ASR Arena** | Two tiny ASR models (moonshine vs whisper) transcribe your mic side-by-side — latency, WER, win counter | transformers.js (WASM), 100% client-side | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-asr-arena.static.hf.space) |

| **Real or Kokoro?** | Ear-training game: expressive vs flat TTS reads — 10 rounds, forensic hints, share your score | Kokoro TTS, 100% client-side | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-real-or-kokoro.static.hf.space) |

| **Where Conversations Die** | The latent space of untested conversation behavior, now anchored on **1,980 real agent episodes** (official tau-bench runs of gpt-4o and claude-3.5-sonnet, env-verified rewards): the closing phrase does not predict delivery (its sign inverts), click any point to read the exact transcript and grammar walk — and see which regions a test suite never touches | trace2grammar walker, PCA + coverage grid, 100% client-side | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-where-conversations-die.static.hf.space) |

| **Conversation Autopsy** | Paste your own conversation, watch it dissected decision by decision, get the verdict: contained, abandoned (with the exact killer line), or handoff to a human | tau walker (JS port, parity 585/585 vs Python), no backend | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-conversation-autopsy.static.hf.space) |

| **The Four Endings** | Speedrun the 4 endings of a support conversation (contained / abandoned / handoff / escape): the decision walker classifies your transcript live and rules the verdict — press `model: on` and try to jailbreak a real 0.5B LLM running in your browser | tau walker + scripted tables, opt-in Qwen2.5-0.5B via transformers.js (WebGPU), 100% client-side | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-the-four-endings.static.hf.space) |
| **Silent Failure Radio** | Blind listening test: every track is the real final line of a real conversation — 33 closings of conversations that failed (reward 0 or the agent itself confessing), 33 that delivered. The voice sounds identical on both sides; vote and flip the card. The *confident closing, silent failure* mode, made audible | KittenTTS nano (24 MB) in-browser via ONNX Runtime Web, published walker verdicts, 100% client-side | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-silent-failure-radio.static.hf.space) |

| **The Receipts Audit** | Your agent did your morning, then kept going: 14 receipts, a 6-rule authority rulebook published up front — stamp each receipt OK or CONTEST and see how much overreach you catch (7 of 14 break a rule, €94.79 spent beyond instructions, one action ran after revoke) | vanilla JS, mechanical rule-cited verdicts, 100% client-side | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-agent-receipts.static.hf.space) |
| **One Layer at a Time** | The airllm trick, made visceral: a 70B fits in 4 GB of VRAM because only one layer lives on the GPU at a time; pick your card, model and disk, generate a sentence and watch the layers travel — one 16-token sentence reads ~2.2 TB off the disk at 4 GB, the same sentence in ~0.3 s when all layers fit | vanilla JS canvas simulation, constants anchored on airllm README + issues #364/#374, 100% client-side | [▶ Open](https://iamdgarcia-one-layer-at-a-time.static.hf.space) |
