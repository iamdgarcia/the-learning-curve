## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an engaging, modern presentation and slide deck framework tailored for interactive workshops, masterclasses, and cohort learning. Drawing direct inspiration from the warm, human, and kinetic signature orange of the brand mark, the aesthetic bridges approachable community energy with high-fidelity editorial clarity.

The visual style combines **Minimalism with Tactile Warmth**:
- **Clarity over Clutter:** Slide canvases maintain generous breathing room to ensure instant legibility from the back of an auditorium or on compressed video streams.
- **Friendly Authority:** Clean geometric structures grounded by soft rounded containers, rich warm-tinted off-white canvases, and deliberate bursts of energetic tangerine and coral.
- **Curated Content Architecture:** Modular layouts tailored specifically for knowledge transmission—portadas de impacto, structured multi-part agendas, side-by-side conceptual frameworks, and interactive exercise cards.

## Colors

The palette balances the radiant vitality of the signature orange against rich dark charcoal and comforting chalk-stone neutrals:

- **Primary (#FF6B00):** The energetic brand core. Used for pivotal slide titles, active slide progress indicators, key action buttons, and primary highlight badges.
- **Secondary (#2B2D42):** Deep slate-navy providing structural gravity, used for secondary anchors, code callouts, and contrast cards.
- **Tertiary (#FFB347):** Soft sunlit amber for step markers, secondary accents, tip callouts, and interactive hover states.
- **Neutral (#1E2022):** Deep charcoal body text for supreme legibility on warm light canvases without the harsh fatigue of pure #000000.
- **Background Tiers:**
  - Base canvas: `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster cream)
  - Slide container / Card surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
  - Subtle divider / Border tone: `#E7E5E0` (Warm slate hairline)
  - Muted secondary text: `#6C727A` (Neutral slate gray)

## Typography

The type system is powered exclusively by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, exploiting its varied optical weights to achieve both bold editorial presence and high information density.

- **Display & Headings:** Rendered with tight negative letter tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and robust weights (`700` and `800`) to evoke an assertive, encouraging voice.
- **Body & Insights:** Engineered with balanced line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to provide effortless scanning across dense presentation blocks.
- **Labels & Badges:** Set in uppercase or small semi-bold caps with positive letter tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) for module tags, session timers, and slide step counters.

## Layout & Spacing

Presentations adhere strictly to a **16:9 widescreen canvas** (`aspect-ratio: 16 / 9`) positioned centrally within an adaptive workspace frame.

- **Slide Stage Grid:** A 12-column dynamic fluid grid system with `1.5rem` gutters and `2.5rem` internal slide margin safeguards content from edge clipping.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Stage Display (Desktop & Fullscreen):** Fixed 16:9 viewport ratio with persistent or auto-hiding top HUD (deck title, module badge) and bottom dock (timeline scrubber, slide number, keyboard hotkeys).
  - **Presenter / Split View (Tablet):** Slide canvas shrinks to 65% with speaker notes and live agenda drawer occupying the remaining vertical or horizontal column.
  - **Mobile Preview:** Unlocks vertical card scrolling where each slide acts as an independent vertical card stacked with `1rem` margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **warm ambient layering and crisp hairlines** rather than synthetic harsh dropshadows:

- **Level 0 (Stage Backdrop):** `#FAF9F6` base canvas with subtle radial illumination centered beneath the active slide.
- **Level 1 (Slide Surface):** Pure `#FFFFFF` slide container finished with a 1px solid `#E7E5E0` hairline border and an ambient diffused perimeter shadow (`0 20px 48px -12px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.06)`).
- **Level 2 (Content Modules & Framework Cards):** `#FAF9F6` or pure `#FFFFFF` inset panels with `0 4px 12px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.03)` and subtle borders (`1px solid #EBE9E4`).
- **Level 3 (Interactive Controls & Floating HUD):** Floating bottom navigation bar and drawer modals leverage backdrop filter blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), translucent fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`), and soft elevation (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(30, 32, 34, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Rounded (Level 2)** geometry:

- **Slide Frame & Canvas:** `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for the main deck card, creating a contemporary app-like presentation experience.
- **Cards, Framework Columns & Exercise Blocks:** `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) offering soft containment for concepts, case studies, and split panels.
- **Buttons, Module Badges & Status Chips:** `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, with module pills retaining full pill circular rounding (`9999px`) to match the curvature of the logo lettering.

## Components

### Slide Navigation & Control Dock
- **Bottom Navigation Dock:** Floating translucent container anchored at the bottom edge. Houses previous/next chevron buttons, slide tracker (`04 / 28`), thumbnail grid modal trigger, and fullscreen toggle.
- **Linear Progress Scrubber:** A continuous 3px bar spanning the top or bottom edge of the slide, with completed segments filled in `#FF6B00` and unvisited sections in `#E7E5E0`.
- **Slide Index Drawer / Modal:** Grid overlay displaying interactive 16:9 thumbnail previews with active slide marked by a 2px primary orange halo.

### Action Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#FF6B00` background, pure `#FFFFFF` bold text, subtle hover lift and color transition to `#E65A00`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent base with 1px border `#D8D5CF`, neutral text `#1E2022`, shifting to warm cream hover `#F4F2EE`.

### Module & Agenda Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Small caps label with 12px horizontal padding and 4px vertical padding. Background set to 12% tint of primary orange (`rgba(255, 107, 0, 0.12)`) paired with `#FF6B00` text.

### Modular Slide Layouts
1. **Title Slide (Portada):** Massive hero headline, high-contrast module pill, workshop facilitator avatar chip, and stylized curved orange accent line.
2. **Agenda & Milestones:** Ordered timeline list with numbered numeric circular badges (`#FF6B00` fill for current topic, `#E7E5E0` for upcoming).
3. **Comparison / Framework Grid:** 2 to 3 vertical cards side-by-side with distinct colored header bands (e.g., "Do vs. Don't", "Concept A vs. Concept B").
4. **Workshop Exercise Card:** Accent border in bold orange, interactive checklist or timer block at top right (`15:00 min remaining`), and high-legibility prompt text.

### Form Inputs & Poll Interactions
- Minimal inputs with `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#D8D5CF` border, and an energetic `#FF6B00` glow outline on `:focus-visible`.
