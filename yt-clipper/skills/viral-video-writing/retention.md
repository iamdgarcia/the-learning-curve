# Retention: keeping people watching after the hook

The hook buys you 3 seconds. Retention is every second after that.
People leave when nothing new happens, so something new has to happen all the time.

## Re-hooks
A re-hook restarts attention before it drops. Put one every **5-8 seconds**.
- **A number:** "and that was only the first week."
- **A turn:** "but then it stopped working."
- **A tease:** "the third one is the one nobody uses."
- **A time jump:** "3 months later."
- **A reveal:** a new screen, a new place, a new person.
- **A question the viewer is already asking,** answered in the next line.

"And this is where it gets crazy" only works if the next thing really is crazy. Otherwise it costs trust.

## Visual pace
- **A new shot every 2-3 seconds.** The 103K story reel has 55 cuts in 2:35, so a new shot every 2.8 seconds.
- **Nothing sits still for more than ~1.5 seconds.** A held screenshot gets a slow push in or a scroll.
- **Every move shows something.** Motion that explains (a number counting, a box landing on the detail) keeps people. Motion for its own sake (wobbling, bouncing, random zooms) looks cheap.
- **One idea per beat.** If two things fight for attention, the viewer gets neither.

## Open loops
Open a question early and close it late.
- "I'll show you the one that made the most money at the end."
- The story version: the villain in the first 10 seconds is the open loop, and the payoff closes it.
Never open a loop you don't close. People notice, and they stop trusting your hooks.

## Pacing the words
- **3.6-4.6 spoken words a second.** Slower sounds like a lecture.
- **Cut every pause down** to about a tenth of a second, and remove breaths.
- **No warm-up lines.** "So basically what I want to talk about today is" goes in the bin.
- **Say it, don't spell it.** The voice says what something does, and the screen shows the exact name, command or number.

## The middle sag
Most videos lose people in the middle, around 40-60%. Fix it with:
- the strongest example in the middle, not at the end
- a pattern break: a new format, a new place, or a quick gag
- a reminder of the promise: "and the last one is the one that got us to $150K"

## The ending
- **End right after the last word.** Dead air at the end lowers watch time.
- **Make it loop.** If the last frame flows into the first, replays count as watch time.
- **One ask.** Follow, comment a keyword, or watch part 2. Never all three.

## How to check a draft
Read the script with a stopwatch:
- [ ] Where is the first re-hook? It should come before 8 seconds.
- [ ] Is there any gap over 8 seconds with no new number, turn, reveal or visual?
- [ ] What's on screen every 2-3 seconds? Write it down.
- [ ] Is the best part in the middle?
- [ ] Does the video end on the last word?
