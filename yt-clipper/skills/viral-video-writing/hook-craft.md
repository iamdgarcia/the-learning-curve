# Hook craft: why a hook works

This file is how to judge a hook and fix it.
The core idea: a hook needs **topic clarity** and **on-target curiosity**.

## A hook has one job
Make the right viewer decide to keep watching. Two things decide it:
1. **Clarity:** they know in 1-2 seconds what this video is about.
2. **Curiosity, aimed at them:** they feel it's for them, and they need the next line.

## The 4 things that kill a hook
| Killer | What it looks like | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Delay** | "Guys, this is one of the craziest things I've ever seen…" | Say the topic in the first 1-2 seconds. Cut every intro. |
| **Confusion** | Clever wording the viewer has to decode | Simple words, short sentences, active voice. Read it out loud. |
| **Irrelevance** | "Three skincare trends." | Talk to them: "If you struggle with acne, try these three things." |
| **No spark** | Clear and relevant, but flat | Add contrast: what people believe vs what's true, or a surprising number. |

## The 2-3 line formula
- **Line 1, the topic:** exactly what the video is about.
- **Line 2-3, the twist:** a contrast, a surprising number or a question that makes them need the rest.

> "Our app made $150K a month and we vibe coded it in 3 days. And no, it wasn't a purple gradient AI app."

## Contrast creates curiosity
- **Stated contrast:** "Most people fix this with X. This works faster."
- **Implied contrast:** "This is 8 times more effective." (8 times more than what? They stay to find out.)
- **Result vs effort:** "This took me 45 minutes. It got 100K views."
- **Insider vs everyone:** "Every top app does this. Almost nobody copies it."

## Visual hook (the first frame)
The words are half of it. On mute, the first frame has to work alone:
- **Something is already moving** on frame 0. No logo, no title card, no "hey guys".
- **Show the payoff first:** the result, the number, the finished thing. Explain after.
- **Hook text on screen** in big type, 8 words or fewer, so it works on mute.
- **One thing to look at.** One centered object or action, not a busy collage.

## Hook checklist (score each one before you write the rest)
- [ ] Topic is clear in under 2 seconds
- [ ] A specific number, name or result is in the first sentence
- [ ] It's aimed at a specific viewer ("you", their problem)
- [ ] There's a contrast or an open question
- [ ] It's true, and the video delivers what it promises
- [ ] The first frame moves and makes sense on mute
- [ ] It's the strongest line in the whole script

Write 5 hooks for every video. Pick the one that passes the most checks, not the one you like best.
