# Tools you can use

You don't need expensive tools to make videos that work. A phone, a free editor and a good script beat a studio with a bad one.
Start with the free column. Only pay when a free tool is actually slowing you down.

## Finding ideas
| Job | Free | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Find outliers on YouTube | The **Popular** tab on any channel, filtered to the last 2-3 months | An outlier is a video doing 5-20x that channel's average. |
| See what's trending on TikTok | **TikTok Creative Center** (trends, hashtags, songs) | Good for sounds and topics, not for copying videos. |
| Hear how your audience talks | **Reddit** search + the comments under big videos | Steal their words for your hooks. |
| Keep a swipe file | Your phone's **Notes** app or a saved folder | Save every video that stopped you, and write one line on why. |

## Writing
| Job | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Draft, score and rewrite | **Claude** + this skill | Paste your idea, get 3 drafts, the checker runs on every one. |
| Get a transcript of any video | Free YouTube transcript sites, or the auto captions in your editor | Needed to break down someone else's video. |
| Check reading level | Any free "readability checker" | Aim for grade 4-6. |

## Filming
| Job | Free / cheap | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Your phone's back camera | Clean the lens. 4K 30fps is plenty. |
| Light | A window in front of you | Face the window, never sit with it behind you. |
| Sound | A cheap clip-on wireless mic | Bad audio makes people swipe faster than bad video. |
| Reading your script | Any free **teleprompter app** | Read it 3 times out loud before you film, so it sounds like talking. |

## Editing
| Job | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cut, captions, music | **CapCut** (free) or the app's own editor | Native edits often feel more real. |
| Edit with Claude | The **famous-reel-editor** skill in this download | One raw clip in, a finished 9:16 reel out. Needs Claude Code. |
| Transcribe with exact word timing | **ElevenLabs Scribe** (paid, best timing) or **Whisper** (free, runs on your computer) | The editor skill uses either one. |
| Covers and thumbnails | **Canva** or **Figma** (free plans) | One subject, a big title, high contrast. Check the 3:4 grid crop. |

## Sound
| Job | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Music that won't get you muted | The platform's own music library, or **Mixkit** (free) | Famous songs can get a video muted or claimed. |
| Paid music library | **Epidemic Sound** or **Artlist** | Worth it once you post every day. |
| Sound effects | **Mixkit** (free) | Subtle and varied. Never the same "ding" on every number. |

## Tracking what works
| Job | Tool | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Views, watch time, where people leave | The **built-in analytics** on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube | Look at the retention graph: the drop shows you the weak line. |
| Track many accounts and videos in one place | **ViewTrack** (made by me) or a simple sheet | Date, hook, format, views at 3 days, comments. After 30 posts you'll see the pattern. |
